# Roadmap

What we are working on and what comes next. There are no dates: Lalabase is built by a small
team, and we would rather ship something when it is good than when a date says so. The order
below is the order we plan to build in, and it can change when we learn something from the
teams that use Lalabase.

What has shipped is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## In progress

### iOS app for meeting recordings

Record a meeting on the iPhone, even without a network connection. The recording arrives in the
right project as a meeting with its transcript. The organisation's glossary makes sure that
technical terms and product names are recognised correctly. The app lives in its own
repository.

## Next, in this order

### 1. Search across all organisations

One search at the top level that finds tickets, comments and knowledge across every client you
work with. No more switching into the right organisation first to find something.

### 2. Conversations with clients

Talk to your client directly in Lalabase, next to tickets and meetings. Questions, agreements
and decisions stay with the project instead of disappearing into personal inboxes.

### 3. Reply by email

Anyone who gets a notification about a conversation or a ticket can simply reply to the email,
and the reply appears in Lalabase. Clients do not have to log in for that.

### 4. Import emails

Every organisation gets its own address. An email forwarded there becomes a conversation with
the client or in the project, so client mail lands where the project lives in a couple of
clicks.

### 5. Link tickets to commits and pull requests

Lalabase picks up references such as `Fixes #187` in commits and pull requests on GitHub and
GitLab and shows them on the ticket, optionally moving the ticket's status when the pull
request is merged. You see on the ticket which code was written for it.

## Later

### More languages

Lalabase speaks German and English today. More languages for the interface are planned.
