# Security Policy

We take the security of Lalabase seriously. Because Lalabase is built for teams
that care about data sovereignty and self-hosting, a vulnerability can directly
affect the confidentiality of our users' data. Thank you for helping keep the
project and its users safe.

## Supported Versions

Lalabase is under active development. Security fixes are provided for:

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | --------- |
| Latest tagged release | ✅ |
| Older releases | ❌ (please upgrade) |

If you self-host, always run the latest tagged release to receive security
fixes.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues,
pull requests, or discussions.**

Instead, report them privately by email to:

> **security@lalabase.com**

If you prefer, you may also use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)
("Report a vulnerability" under the repository's **Security** tab).

To help us triage quickly, please include where you can:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept, affected endpoint/component, request samples)
- The affected version, commit, or deployment type (Cloud vs. self-hosted)
- Any suggested mitigation, if you have one

## What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement** within **3 business days** of your report.
- An initial **assessment and severity classification** within **10 business days**.
- We will keep you informed about remediation progress and coordinate a
  disclosure timeline with you.
- With your consent, we are happy to **credit you** once a fix is released.

## Disclosure Policy

We follow **coordinated disclosure**. Please give us a reasonable opportunity to
remediate before any public disclosure — ordinarily up to **90 days** from your
report, or sooner once a fix has shipped. (This 90-day disclosure window is
separate from the response times above, which describe how quickly *we* get back
to *you*.)

To the extent permitted by applicable law, we will not initiate or support legal
action against security researchers who:

- Act in good faith and avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and the
  disruption or degradation of our services,
- Only access, modify, or interact with data or accounts they own or have
  explicit permission to test, and
- Give us the disclosure window above to remediate before going public.

This policy is the authorization boundary for security testing of Lalabase: it
does **not** authorize actions against third-party services, infrastructure, or
data we do not control, and it does not waive any rights of third parties.

Thank you for contributing to the security of Lalabase.
